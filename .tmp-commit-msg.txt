fix(rich-blocks): split line-initial anchors out of their paragraph (md4x keeps them as inline html)

Live T10 found it: an <a name="id"></a> line followed by text WITHOUT a
blank line (common LLM output) arrived as ONE paragraph with the anchor as
inline html nodes and rendered literal. Split the paragraph at the anchor
pair (paragraph-before / anchor / paragraph-after, line-edges trimmed) —
old line-parser parity. Mid-line anchors stay literal (the old line regex
was line-anchored too). A stray </a> continuation line now stays literal
raw data instead of being silently consumed.
