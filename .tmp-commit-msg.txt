fix(rich-blocks): draft sanitizer for the streaming edge (live T10 flicker)

heal has two rough edges at the streaming edge (owner-verified in the live
draft): a trailing LONE backtick (odd total count) is doubled into a visible
literal "``", and a trailing lone */~ (incomplete closing run, e.g. "**bold *")
stays literal. Both leak raw markers into the draft for a tick or two.

sanitizeDraftTail (drafts only — the heal path of mdToBlocksV2) strips:
- a trailing backtick run when the total backtick count is odd (the unclosed
  opener of a streaming doc is always the newest one), and
- a trailing lone * or ~ when that marker's count is odd (half-typed closer;
  even counts are literal text and stay).
Completed spans are untouched (even counts), persistent text never passes
through, and mid-text unclosed constructs are left to heal (it closes them
properly).
